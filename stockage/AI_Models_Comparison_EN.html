Note: All these models have multimodal capabilities (they can understand text, audio, image or code).

Gemini 2.5 Pro

Gemini 2.5 Pro is Google's premium model, fast, versatile, capable of simultaneously handling text, images and native multimodality. Excels in narration, long dialogue and multilingual creative generation. For professional uses, but its pricing is one of the highest on the market.

Claude Sonnet 4.5

Claude Sonnet 4.5 from Anthropic shines through stylistic finesse, dialogue coherence and mastery of scientific content, including code. Natural conversation and good stability, particularly for dialogue-oriented uses. Its speed may vary.

Qwen3-30B

Qwen3-30B, developed by Alibaba, is a powerful open source Mixture of Experts (MoE) model that stands out in long context management, mathematical reasoning, coding and multilingual flexibility. Cost is very competitive.

Grok 4 Fast Reasoning

Grok 4 Fast Reasoning specializes in complex reasoning, real-time web access and rapid technical content generation. It offers high-performance multi-step reasoning, good context management. Less suited to highly creative narratives, it remains an excellent choice for monitoring, code and technical synthesis.

DeepSeek Chat

DeepSeek Chat is both efficient and affordable. A fluid, natural model, adapted to varied and narrative styles. It handles creative aspects and storytelling well. Good contextual understanding for synthesizing, summarizing, reformulating, and responding in depth to literary analysis or criticism.

GPT-5

GPT-5, OpenAI's latest model, offers exceptional literary and scientific quality thanks to deep multimodal understanding, advanced reasoning and enhanced security. It marks innovation and universality, but its price is the highest and has restrictions depending on usage.

Kimi2

Kimi2 is an AI oriented towards dialogue, narration and multi-agent work. Good adaptive capacity, effective code and scientific content management, at a very competitive price, but lags behind on the most advanced multimodal fusion and certain premium use cases.
